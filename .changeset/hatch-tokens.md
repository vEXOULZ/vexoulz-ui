Hatch tokens for the diagonal-stripe patterns: `--vx-hatch` (light stripes), `--vx-hatch-dark` (dark stripes over a colour), `--vx-hatch-bad` (error stripes) and `--vx-hatch-faint` (the placeholder fill). The placeholder and the restricted chapter in `.vx-chapterbar` now use them; the restricted stripe is `--vx-ink` instead of pure white (#f2f2f2 vs #fff at 35%).
